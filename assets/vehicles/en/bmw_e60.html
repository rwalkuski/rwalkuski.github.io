BMW 5-series E60/E61

Available parameters:

  • RPM
  • Coolant temperature
  • Oil temperature (if available)
  • Actual gear (P/R/N/D/S/M) and gear number (A/T only)
  • Gearbox oil temperature (A/T only)
  • Voltage
  • Torque (kinda accurate)

Additional features:

  • Dimming via OE dimmer
  • Automatic detection of engine and transmission

Wiring (in progress):

Connect +12V (red wire) to terminal 30g in the vehicle

Connect GND (black wire) to metal surface.

Connect CANH (green wire) to blue/red wire.

Connect CANL (orange/brown wire) to blue wire.
