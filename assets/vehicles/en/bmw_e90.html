BMW 3-series E90/E91/E92/E93

Available parameters:

  • RPM
  • Coolant temperature
  • Oil temperature (if available)
  • Actual gear (P/R/N/D/S/M) and gear number (A/T only)
  • Gearbox oil temperature (A/T only)
  • Voltage
  • Torque (kinda accurate)

Additional features:

  • Dimming via OE dimmer
  • Automatic detection of engine and transmission

Wiring:

Connect +12V (red wire) to Fuse 13 in the car. If fuse not empty, place fuse from the car into empty slot in fuse holder you've got.

Connect GND (black wire) to metal surface.

Connect CANH (green wire) to blue/red wire. (connector X14271, pin 1)

Connect CANL (orange/brown wire) to blue wire. (connector X14271, pin 2)
